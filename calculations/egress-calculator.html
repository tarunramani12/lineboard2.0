Supabase (checked 2026-11-15)

Supabase's own docs list $0.09 per GB uncached and $0.03 per GB cached, and Pro includes 250 GB. You asked for no free allowance, so the defaults are 0 free GB, 0% cached and no fixed fee, which is the worst case for egress. To see the Pro plan, set Free GB to 250 and the fixed fee to 25. If pictures are served from Supabase's cache, raise the cached share to see the lower cost.

Selling costs

The provider fee is taken after VAT is removed, which matches the earlier plan (Micro keeps about €0.69). Paddle's page says 5% + 50 cents, quoted in dollars; I treat it as euros here. Polar's headline is 4% + 40 cents, with about 0.5% extra for subscriptions and 1.5% extra for non-US cards, and Polar's newer plan structure may differ, so enter the current figures from the live page if you choose Polar. Monthly payments only: a yearly payment loses less to the fixed fee.

Other fixed costs default to €9 because the earlier estimate of €25 to 50 a month assumed Supabase Pro; set the Supabase fixed fee to 25 and the free GB to 250 to include it.

Per-plan people and egress factors in the table are my guesses: a bigger plan means a bigger project and more people, and the factor scales how much each person downloads compared with Micro (1.0).
